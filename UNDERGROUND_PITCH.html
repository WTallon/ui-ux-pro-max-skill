WHAT UNDERGROUND IS

Not a track ID app. Not a ticketing platform. Not a social network.
The OS — where every part of the scene lives in one place, and everything else becomes a feature.

Community first. Software second. Culture always.

Entry Point A — The Wedge

TRACK IDs + EVENTS

"What track was that?" and "What's happening tonight?" are the two questions EDM fans ask every single week. Underground answers both better than anything else.

Market as: "The best place to discover IDs, track what DJs are playing, and find EDM events."
Entry Point B — The Scene

SOCIAL + CULTURE

For fans who already know they want a social network built for the scene. Post set reviews, festival clips, hot takes, rankings, predictions. Follow DJs. Build status.

Market as: "Instagram for EDM. Built by the scene, for the scene."

Both entry points lead to the same experience: a feed, a community, and an identity in the scene. The wedge users discover the social layer. The social users discover the track ID and radar tools. Over time there is no difference.

Section 08

HEAT SCORE

A proprietary 0–100 score assigned to every track on Underground — released or unreleased. It's what makes the Radar unique. It's what tells you this track is about to be everywhere before it's everywhere.

WHAT FEEDS IT

ID requests. DJ plays via Setlist Intelligence. Fan saves. City activity. Reposts. Festival appearances. Every interaction across the platform moves a track's score.

HOW IT WORKS

Scores decay without activity — a track only stays hot while the scene is actually talking about it. Scores reset when a track goes truly mainstream. An animated ring appears in the UI at Heat Score 90+.

WHY IT MATTERS

A track on Bandcamp being played at Berghain will rank high even if it's not on Spotify yet. Underground sees what the scene hears — not what the algorithm promotes.

The Heat Score is Underground's Rotten Tomatoes. The number fans quote when they talk about a track. "That ID has a Heat Score of 97 — it's going to drop within the month." Labels watch it. Booking agents watch it. Fans brag about following tracks before they peaked. The Heat Score makes the prediction game real.

Section 14

REVENUE MODEL

Seven distinct revenue streams, unlocked in order as the community grows. Community adoption unlocks each tier — not the other way around.

01

Subscriptions

Fan Pass, DJ Pro, Venue Pro, Label Pro. Free remains genuinely useful — Pro adds power, not access.

02

Ticketing Commission

Venues list events and sell tickets directly through the platform. Underground takes a small fee per ticket. Venues receive money directly — Underground never holds funds.

03

Promoted Placement

Venues and promoters pay for featured event placement. Feed boost, city page top, Tonight Near Me pin. Self-serve dashboard. No sales team needed.

04

Booking Marketplace

Underground takes a percentage of confirmed bookings. DJs and promoters transact directly through the platform.

05

Affiliate Links

DJ gear, DAWs, sample packs, plugins. Contextual placement on DJ profiles, setlist exports, track pages. These users already buy this stuff.

06

Brand & Festival Advertising

Ultra, Tomorrowland, Pioneer DJ, Roland, and others. Sponsored city pages, feed takeovers, event spotlights. Unlocks at scale.

07

Intelligence Reports

Anonymized trend data licensed to labels, booking agencies, and festivals. "Which tracks are getting the most ID requests in Miami?" No external source can replicate this.
